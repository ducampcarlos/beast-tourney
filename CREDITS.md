## Prior art

Not used as code or assets, but the inspiration for how this plays: [Super Auto Pets](https://teamwood.games/super-auto-pets) (the shop, the merging and the one-by-one clash), Teamfight Tactics and Auto Chess (faction bonds), and PokéRogue and Cassette Beasts for what makes a creature-collecting game worth replaying. No Pokémon or other trademarked characters appear anywhere in this project: every creature and name here is original.
